EditMaster Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 20, 2026

EditMaster is a photo and video(basic) editing application that allows users to edit photos and videos, apply effects, use AI-powered photo editing features, and export edited images or videos to their device gallery.

1. Information We Collect

Google account information: If you choose to sign in with Google, EditMaster may receive basic account information made available through Google Sign-In, such as your name, email address, as permitted by your Google account settings and the permissions requested by the app.

Photos and videos: EditMaster accesses photos and videos that you select or authorize for editing. Photos are used to provide editing, effects, AI features, and export functionality. We do not access unrelated photos or videos for purposes unrelated to the app's editing functions.

Technical information: The app may process technical information needed for app operation, security, diagnostics, and performance, including device type, operating-system information, app version, and crash/diagnostic information, if such services are enabled in the released app.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide photo or video editing, effects, AI editing(only available for photo), and export features.
  • To provide Google Sign-In and maintain the user's EditMaster account, if sign-in is used.
  • To save and restore projects or settings when such functionality is enabled.
  • To maintain security, troubleshoot errors, and improve performance.

3. AI-Powered Features

AI features are available only for photos as of now. EditMaster may provide AI-powered features such as background removal and other image-editing functions. When you use these AI features, the selected image or image data is securely transmitted over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection to our backend processing provider, Emergent, which utilizes Google Gemini models to perform the requested edits.

Images transmitted for AI processing are used solely to fulfill your editing request, are processed transiently, and are not retained for model training or permanent storage.

4. Google Services and Third Parties

To facilitate optional user authentication and account access it uses Google Sign-In.

To provide cloud-based AI image generation and editing capabilities it uses Emergent & Google AI (Gemini)

5. Photo or Video Processing and Storage

Photo or video editing may be performed locally on your device. Edited images or videos can be exported to your device's gallery when you choose to do so. EditMaster does not claim ownership of your photos or videos.

If a feature requires server-side processing, the relevant data may temporarily leave your device as described in this policy.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Photos and videos selected for ordinary local editing remain strictly under your control on your device. When server-side processing is used for an AI feature, image data is processed ephemerally and retained only for the duration necessary to execute the request.

If you sign in with Google, you may delete your account and all associated account data at any time directly within the app settings.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to the nature of the information processed. Data transmitted to services should use secure, encrypted connections.

8. Children's Privacy

EditMaster is not specifically directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so that appropriate action can be taken.

9. Your Choices

  • You can choose which photos or videos to provide to the app.
  • You can choose whether to use optional AI editing features for photos.
  • You can choose whether to sign in with Google(optional).
  • You can remove exported images or videos from your device using your device's normal gallery/file controls.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our app, services, or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published at this URL with a revised effective date.
