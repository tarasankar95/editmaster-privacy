EditMaster Account & Data Deletion

If you created an EditMaster account using Google Sign-In and want your EditMaster account and associated account data deleted, contact us using the privacy email below.

Email: ts.banerjee.it@gmail.com

In your request, use the subject EditMaster Account Deletion Request and provide the Google account email used with EditMaster so that we can identify the correct account.

We will process the request in accordance with applicable law and our Privacy Policy. Data that we are legally required to retain may be retained for the applicable period.

Local photos and exported files stored on your Android device are not deleted by an EditMaster account deletion request; you can delete those files using your device's normal gallery/file controls.
